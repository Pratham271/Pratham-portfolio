## How to describe my taste

> My reading moves between Manto, Tagore, Indian history and civilisation, meaning and psychology, and technical books on machine learning, statistics, and distributed systems. Very light beach reading, obviously.

Do not invent an exact ranking or claim that one book is my single favourite.
